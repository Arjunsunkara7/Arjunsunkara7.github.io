Academic Performance Indicator

Semester-wise structured evidence record of teaching, co-curricular and research contributions — maintained as part of St. Mary's College annual faculty appraisal framework.

About the API Framework: The Academic Performance Indicator (API) is St. Mary's College's structured self-appraisal system. Faculty contributions are assessed across three categories — B(I) Teaching, Learning & Evaluation, B(II) Co-Curricular, Extension & Professional Development, and B(III) Research, Knowledge Creation & Institution Building. Records are maintained semester-wise (Odd: July–November · Even: December–April). Scores and proof links will be updated at the close of each semester.
