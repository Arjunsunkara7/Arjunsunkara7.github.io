An Intentional Academic Journey

Dr. Nagarjuna Sunkara is an interdisciplinary academic and researcher anchored in Management and Business Analytics, with complementary foundations in psychology, counselling and social work.

His path spans over a decade of corporate HR leadership, a doctoral programme focused on graduate employability, and active engagement with community organisations working on inclusion and social development. He currently teaches at St. Mary's College, Hyderabad.
