About

Interdisciplinary Academic & Researcher — People, Organisations & Communities

Biography

An Intentional Academic Journey

Dr. Nagarjuna Sunkara is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Business Management at St. Mary's College, Yousufguda, Hyderabad. His academic and professional identity is anchored in Management and Business Analytics, enriched by an intentionally built interdisciplinary foundation spanning general psychology, counselling and psychotherapy, and social work.

His journey into academia was shaped by over a decade of corporate HR leadership across the technology, manufacturing and consulting sectors — experience that continues to inform how he approaches teaching, research and student mentoring. Working across organisations of varying scale, he developed deep expertise in HR systems, workforce analytics, talent management and organisational development before returning to full-time doctoral research.

His PhD, completed in 2022 at Acharya Nagarjuna University, examined the employability skills of management graduates from the perspectives of employers, educators and students — a question that sits at the intersection of higher education, industry expectations and social mobility. This research continues to shape his scholarly interests in graduate readiness, skill development and the evolving nature of work.

Dr. Sunkara's interdisciplinary qualifications in psychology, counselling and social work are not peripheral to his academic identity — they deepen his understanding of human behaviour, organisational life and community well-being, and reflect a sustained commitment to approaching management and analytics through a human-centred lens.

At St. Mary's College, he brings real-world datasets, industry case studies and evidence-informed approaches into the BBA classroom, teaching Business Analytics, Business Economics and Advanced Data Visualisation. He is an active class mentor and is committed to developing students' analytical thinking, professional confidence and career readiness.

Beyond the classroom, Dr. Sunkara has been actively engaged with community-based organisations working on LGBTQIA+ inclusion, educational outreach and youth development — a dimension of his professional life that he regards as integral rather than incidental to his work as an educator and researcher.

Research Philosophy

What Drives My Research

I believe research should begin with questions that matter to people, organisations and society. My research is driven by an interest in understanding how workplaces, technology, organisational practices and human behaviour influence individual and organisational outcomes.

I find myself particularly drawn to questions around employability, skill development, psychological safety, inclusive workplaces, people analytics and the changing nature of work in the age of artificial intelligence. I aim to produce research that goes beyond academic publication and contributes to practical improvements in organisational decision-making, employee well-being, educational practices and career readiness.

Ultimately, I want my research to bridge the gap between theory and practice and create knowledge that is meaningful, relevant and capable of making a positive difference in people's lives.

Beyond the Classroom

Community Engagement

I believe that education, inclusion and community development are interconnected. My commitment to community engagement extends beyond the classroom through participation in community-based organisations, educational outreach and youth mentoring.

Meaningful social change begins with inclusion, education and empowerment. Whether through community leadership, school outreach or individual mentoring, I aim to create accessible learning opportunities.
