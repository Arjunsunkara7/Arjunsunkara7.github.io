Teaching Philosophy

My Approach to Teaching

"I believe great teaching is about creating experiences that challenge students to think critically, engage authentically and grow professionally. My classroom is a space where theory meets practice — where management concepts are not just taught but explored through real-world data, industry case studies and collaborative problem-solving. I bring my industry experience into the classroom, helping students bridge the gap between academic learning and professional expectations. I aim to develop not just knowledgeable graduates but confident, analytical and empathetic future professionals who are ready to lead in complex, evolving organisations."

Pedagogy

How I Teach

Real-World Datasets

Classes are designed around live and real-world datasets drawn from industry sources, enabling students to work with the same quality of data they will encounter in professional settings.

Case-Based Learning

Each concept is anchored in an industry case study — from HR analytics in FMCG to econometric models in financial services — making abstract theory immediately actionable.

Collaborative Problem-Solving

Group projects and structured seminars encourage peer learning, teamwork and the kind of professional communication that employers consistently identify as a gap among fresh graduates.

Analytical Thinking First

Tools are taught as means, not ends. Whether Excel, Python, Tableau or SPSS, the emphasis is always on the quality of the question being asked, not the software being used to answer it.

Student Development

Mentoring & Academic Contributions

Class Mentoring

As a class mentor for BBA students, I work closely with students on academic performance, career planning, skill development and overall well-being. Mentoring is a responsibility I take seriously — it is an extension of teaching, not an administrative duty.

Career Readiness

I facilitate regular sessions on career planning, resume writing, interview skills and professional communication — drawing directly on my industry background in HR and talent management to give students practical, current guidance.
